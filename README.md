## Hey, I'm Alex! 👋

I am a Research Fellow at the Industrial Robotics Facility of the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia, where I develop perception and motion-planning systems for autonomous mobile manipulators in industrial applications. My current work focuses primarily on simulation, system development, and validation.

I received my degree in Electrical Engineering from Universidad Central de Venezuela, where my thesis focused on reinforcement learning for robotic manipulation. I also taught PIC microcontroller programming and digital circuit design before working as a Software Engineer and later transitioning into robotics research.

I am interested in developing fully autonomous robotic systems for industrial applications, using simulation to design, test, and validate robotic capabilities for deployment on real-world robots.

📖 In my free time, I share what I am learning on my [blog](https://alefram.github.io/).
